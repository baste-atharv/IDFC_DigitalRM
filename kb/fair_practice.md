# Fair practice and consent rules for connectors
Status: summary written for this demo; check the bank's connector code of conduct before use.

## Why this matters
[SOURCED] In FY 2025-26, "Recovery Agents / Direct Sales Agents" were the largest complaint ground at IDFC FIRST Bank, at 30% of complaints. Good conduct by connectors protects customers, the bank and the connector's own payouts.

## Customer consent
Never share a customer's details or documents with the bank without the customer's permission. [SOURCED] Under RBI's Digital Lending Directions 2025, customer data is collected only with explicit consent, and the customer can deny or revoke consent. The DPDP Act 2023 also needs a lawful basis and a stated purpose.

## Never do this
Never change, edit or "adjust" salary slips, bank statements, ITRs or any document. Never promise a loan approval, an interest rate or a waiver: only the bank decides. Never collect any fee or cash from the customer: the bank pays the connector. Never use threats or pressure. Never ask a customer for their OTP or password.

## Always do this
Tell the customer you are a connector for IDFC FIRST Bank. Explain that rates and approval depend on the bank's assessment. Give the customer the bank's official channels for questions. Report any request to bend rules to your RM.

## Complaints and escalation
[SOURCED] A customer must first complain to the bank; if there is no reply within 30 days or the complaint is rejected, they may approach the RBI Ombudsman. Connectors should help customers reach the bank's grievance channel quickly.

## About this assistant
This assistant is an AI: the digital assistant of your RM. It can be wrong. For disputes, sensitive issues or large loans, it connects you to your human RM. [SOURCED] RBI's FREE-AI framework expects customers to be told when they deal with AI and to have a way to reach a human.
