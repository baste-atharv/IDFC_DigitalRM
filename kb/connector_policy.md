## How payouts work
[SOURCED] MyFIRST Partner pays connectors on disbursal, weekly, directly to the connector's bank account. In this demo, payouts are credited every Friday for disbursals cleared by the previous Monday. Payout is a percentage of the disbursed amount, for example 1.5% for personal loans and 0.4% for home loans (illustrative grid), or a flat Rs 500 per issued credit card.

## Why a payout can be on hold
A payout goes on hold when the GST invoice is not uploaded (for GST-registered connectors), when the bank account name does not match the connector's PAN name, or when the customer cancels the loan within 15 days (the payout is then clawed back). Fix the hold reason in the app and the payout moves to the next Friday cycle.

## Tiers and rewards
Tiers are based on disbursals in the last 90 days: Bronze (0-1), Silver (2-4, payout x1.05), Gold (5-9, payout x1.10), Platinum (10+, payout x1.15). Illustrative.

## Lead status stages
Submitted, Documents pending, Login (file with credit team), Sanctioned, Disbursed, or Rejected. Documents pending is the most common reason a lead stops moving.

## Raising an issue
Raise a ticket in the app for payout delay, lead stuck, wrong payout amount or login problems. The RM is notified. Tickets open for more than 7 days are escalated.
