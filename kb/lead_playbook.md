# Lead-generation playbook by connector type
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE ideas for the demo.

## CA / tax consultant
Clients filing returns are business owners and professionals. Ask which clients are expanding, buying equipment or paying off costly loans: business loan, professional loan or LAP. Tax season (January to March) and GST filing days are natural moments to ask.

## Real-estate broker
Every buyer of a home is a home-loan lead; every seller who owns a second property may need LAP. Ask buyers about financing on the first site visit. Festive season (October to December) brings bookings.

## Insurance advisor
Salaried families need personal loans for weddings, education loans for children and credit cards. Policy renewal calls are a natural moment to ask about upcoming expenses.

## Car dealer sales executive
Every walk-in buyer who is not paying cash is a used-car or two-wheeler loan lead. Offer financing at the test drive. Festive season and month-end are peaks.

## Shop owner / trader
Fellow traders need working capital before festivals and wedding season. Regular customers may need two-wheeler or personal loans.

## Coaching institute owner
Admission season (April to July) brings education loan needs for students going to college or abroad. Parent seminars are a good moment.

## Clinic / pharmacy owner
Doctors setting up or expanding clinics need professional loans; chemists need working capital. Medical conferences and association meetings are good moments.

## How to ask without being pushy
Ask about plans, not loans: "Any big expense coming up this year?" Share one simple message, then follow up once. Always take consent before sharing details.
