# Product library for connectors
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE demo library. Lines marked [SOURCED] come from public IDFC FIRST material; everything else is a placeholder the bank replaces with its own product sheets. Rates and amounts are indicative; final terms depend on the bank's credit assessment.

## Personal Loan
For salaried and self-employed individuals for weddings, travel, medical needs, home renovation or debt consolidation. [SOURCED, bank blog 2021] Loan amounts from Rs 20,000 to Rs 40 lakh, tenure 6 to 60 months. Indicative eligibility: age 23+, stable monthly income, good repayment history. Documents: PAN, Aadhaar or address proof, last 3 months salary slips or bank statements. [SOURCED] The MyFIRST Partner app asks for PAN, address proof and the latest 3 months' bank statement for soft approval.

## Business Loan (unsecured)
[SOURCED, FAME 6.0 PS5] Unsecured business loans from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 1 crore with 1 to 7 year tenure, no collateral, for business expansion, working capital and debt consolidation. Best for shop owners, traders and small manufacturers with 3+ years of business vintage and filed income tax returns. Documents: PAN, GST registration, 2 years ITR, 12 months business bank statement.

## Professional Loan
[SOURCED, FAME 6.0 PS5] For doctors, salaried doctors, chartered accountants, architects, company secretaries and other qualified professionals, who have stable incomes and lower credit risk. Market lending rates are typically 10% to 11%. Uses: clinic or office setup, equipment, working capital. Documents: degree or registration certificate, PAN, ITR, bank statement.

## Home Loan
For buying a ready or under-construction home, self-construction or balance transfer. Long tenures (up to 20-30 years, illustrative). Documents: KYC, income proof, property papers, sale agreement. Real-estate brokers are the natural source.

## Loan Against Property (LAP)
Secured loan against a residential or commercial property for business expansion, education or balance transfer. Customer segments include MSMEs, education institutions, salaried and self-employed professionals, and NRIs. Needs a technical valuation and legal check of the property.

## Used Car Loan
For pre-owned car purchases through dealers or private sale. Quick decisions when the car's registration and insurance are in order. Car dealer sales executives are the natural source.

## Two-Wheeler Loan
Small-ticket loan for scooters and motorcycles, often with low down payment. Good for first-time borrowers.

## Education Loan
For higher studies in India or abroad: tuition, hostel, travel, laptop. Co-applicant (parent) usually needed. Coaching institute owners meet students at admission time (April to July).

## Credit Card
For salaried and self-employed customers with a good credit history. Connector payout is a flat amount per issued card (illustrative).
